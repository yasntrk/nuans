Reviewer notes.

The app needs camera access to work at all; there is nothing to see without it.

To exercise the measurement without a patient:
1. Skip the device profile calibration when prompted. The app will run with a
   manufacturer colour matrix and show a low-confidence badge, which is the
   intended behaviour rather than an error.
2. On the camera screen, point it at any printed matte surface. The capture
   button stays disabled until all six live checks pass; this is deliberate and
   is explained on screen by the failing check.
3. Place any neutral grey card in the dashed slot beside the tooth outline to
   satisfy the grey card check.

The app stores data on the device only and makes no network requests.

It is a clinical decision support tool for dental professionals. It does not
diagnose, and it states on the result screen and in every exported report that
the final shade choice remains the clinician's responsibility.
