Nuans turns a phone camera into a calibrated colour measurement tool for dentists and dental technicians.

WHAT IT DOES
• Measures the tooth in three regions: cervical, body, incisal
• Matches against standard shade guides and reports the three closest shades with ΔE00
• Proposes mixing proportions and a layering plan when one shade is not enough
• Produces separate output for zirconia, metal-ceramic and composite
• Builds a bilingual PDF report, with the report language chosen independently of the app language

HOW IT MEASURES
• RAW capture with manual exposure, locked focus and locked white balance
• A grey card in every capture anchors the white point and the absolute lightness
• A one-time per-device calibration against a colour reference chart
• CIELAB (D65, 2°) and CIEDE2000, with thresholds consistent with ISO/TR 28642

HONEST LIMITS
A phone camera is not a spectrophotometer. It measures three channels, not a spectrum; it cannot see metamerism and it estimates translucency. Every measurement carries a confidence score, and every condition that lowers it is shown plainly. The shade values that ship with the app are not measurements: a clinic is expected to measure its own physical guide.

DATA
Works entirely offline. Patient images are stored encrypted on the device only. There is no cloud sync. Records are keyed by a patient code, never by a name. Deletion and export are supported.

A clinical decision support tool. The final shade choice remains the responsibility of the clinician or technician.

Brand names are used only to state compatibility; the app is not affiliated with any shade guide manufacturer.
